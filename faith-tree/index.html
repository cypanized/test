About this tree

Dates marked “c.” are approximate, and many are debated. The time scale widens at 1000 BCE, 1 CE and 1500 CE so the crowded recent centuries stay readable.

Real traditions overlap, borrow and merge in ways no tree can fully show. This one follows common historical accounts and leaves out many smaller groups and most Indigenous traditions, which are older than records or didn’t branch in documented ways.

Many communities describe their own origins differently. Hindus and Jains see their traditions as eternal. Latter-day Saints, Baháʼís, Sikhs and others see a restoration or a new revelation rather than a split.
