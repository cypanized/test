Pope Leo from Rue de Berri

Saturday 26 September 2026 · Paris, 8th arrondissement

Seeing Pope Leo XIV from Rue de Berri

The open-air papal Mass runs along the Champs-Élysées to Place de la Concorde. It is free and open without a ticket while space lasts. Your hotel sits on the edge of the security zone, about 260 m from the avenue.

Paris time Official gates closed at 13:00. The popemobile comes down the Champs-Élysées from 13:45, and Mass is 15:00–16:30. Try the two routes below right away.
Route 1 · first try this

Walk down Rue de Berri to the Champs-Élysées

Head down Rue de Berri toward Rue de Ponthieu (to your left as you step out). If there is no police barrier between you and the avenue, you can walk straight to it. Stand near the corner of Rue de Berri.

260 m · about 4 min
Route 2 · if a barrier blocks you

Walk up to checkpoint “Entrée D Nord”

It's at the corner of Rue de Berri and Rue d'Artois. Expect a bag search. Gates officially closed at 13:00, so ask the officers whether they're still letting people in. Show your hotel key or booking.

120 m · about 2 min

Your two routes

  • Popemobile route, toward Concorde
  • Security zone

The zone outline is traced from the official Île-de-France Mobilités / Préfecture de Police map and snapped to OpenStreetMap streets. The line follows Rue de Berri between Rue de Ponthieu and Rue d'Artois, right at your door. Treat it as a guide and follow what officers tell you on the spot.

Where you are on the Pope's route

The altar is about 1.5 km from the corner of Rue de Berri, too far to see directly. About forty giant screens line the avenue. Your best chance to see the Pope in person is when the popemobile passes on its way to Concorde.

At the security check

Allowed

  • Small backpack, under 25 litres
  • Water bottle or thermos, up to 1.5 L
  • Food and a picnic
  • Umbrella, cane, crutches
  • Pushchair for a child under 6

Refused at the gate

  • Suitcases or bags over 25 L (there is no bag storage)
  • Glass, alcohol, anything that could be a weapon
  • Bikes, scooters, e-bike batteries and helmets
  • Folding chairs (except for elderly or disabled people)
  • Drones, animals other than assistance dogs, political signs

If you can't get in

Getting around

  • Line 1 is closed at Étoile, George V, Franklin D. Roosevelt, Champs-Élysées–Clemenceau and Concorde until the end of the day
  • Ternes and Saint-Philippe-du-Roule are closed from 12:00
  • For the north gates, use Courcelles (line 2) or Miromesnil (lines 9, 13). Each is about 10 min on foot
  • No bikes or scooters inside the zone

After Paris the Pope goes to Lourdes (26–27 Sept) and Metz (Mon 28 Sept).
