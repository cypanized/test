# GuitarZero2Hero intro reel

`gz2h-reel.html` puts every intro cut on one page. A silent showreel at the top plays them back to back. Under it, each cut has a card with its idea, its sound and a link to its player with sound. `media/` holds the 960×540 silent preview loops and poster stills the page uses.
